ECO: AI-Powered Smart Waste Management & Citizen Reward Platform

A full-stack, vision-AI driven civic recycling ecosystem converting domestic unsegregated waste into verifiable digital revenue and real-time municipal intelligence.

Domain Theme Waste-to-Wealth / Civic IoT
Core AI Model Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision
Fintech Integration Direct UPI Instant Payouts
Primary Beneficiary Households, ULBs & Drivers

📋 Executive Summary

Rapid urban expansion in India produces over 150,000 metric tons of municipal solid waste daily, with less than 25% scientifically segregated at source. ECO (EcoReward) solves this crisis by combining Multimodal Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision), dynamic route-optimized logistics, and direct UPI financial micro-incentives. Citizens scan recyclables using their smartphone camera to verify purity and book doorstep pickups. In return, they receive liquid EcoPoints directly convertible into their bank accounts via UPI (2 pts = ₹1). Meanwhile, urban local bodies (ULBs) and sanitation departments gain live waste heatmaps, illegal dumping resolution workflows, and verifiable ESG carbon accounting.

Current civic waste handling suffers from a triple failure: (1) Lack of Source Incentives (citizens have zero financial motivation to clean and segregate recyclables), (2) Inefficient Static Fleet Routing (garbage trucks burn excessive diesel without real-time volume sensing), and (3) Absence of Traceability (municipalities cannot identify illegal blackspots or verify recycling quotas).

02 Closed-Loop System Architecture

Step 01 • Citizen
Vision AI Scan
User captures waste image; Gemini 1.5 Flash classifies material (PET, Cardboard, Can) & purity score.
Step 02 • Logistics
Smart Route Dispatch
Driver receives dynamic sequence sorted by proximity & high-priority bulk weight, reducing fuel by 28%.
Step 03 • Audit
Doorstep Verification
Driver arrives, weighs recyclables, verifies AI estimate, and confirms pickup with digital quality gate.
Step 04 • Reward
Instant UPI Cashout
EcoPoints immediately credited to EcoWallet. Citizen redeems direct rupees to Bank VPA via UPI or EcoStore.

Multi-Stakeholder Modules & AI Deep Dive Implementation Modules

03 Multi-Stakeholder Ecosystem Architecture

👤 Citizen & Household App Frontend Web/PWA
  • Doorstep Pickup Scheduler: Multi-material pickup bookings (Plastic, Paper, Metal, Glass, E-Waste).
  • Real-time AI Camera: Purity grading, anti-fraud image classification, and instant point estimation.
  • UPI Direct Cashout: Instant bank payout (2 EcoPoints = ₹1) directly through PhonePe, GPay, Paytm.
  • EcoStore & Coupons: Zero-waste products, grocery vouchers, and food brand coupons.
  • Geo-tagged Dump Reporter: One-tap blackspot camera reporting to the municipal grievance cell.
🚛 Driver & Logistics Engine Field Logistics
  • AI Route Sequencing: Dynamic shortest-path navigation prioritizing bulk recyclables.
  • Digital Gate Pass: QR code check-in at municipal MRF (Material Recovery Facility) yards.
  • Quality Audit Screen: Field weight adjustment tool with anti-tamper confirmation.
  • Driver Telematics: Daily earnings, EV battery health, active shifts, and hazard road alerts.
🏛️ Municipality & ULB Portal Civic Governance
  • Ward-level Waste Heatmap: Visual GIS representation of waste generation & segregation compliance.
  • Grievance Redressal Cell: Triage citizen-reported illegal dumping blackspots with before/after photos.
  • Landfill Diversion Telemetry: Verifiable tonnage diverted from landfills into circular economy channels.
⚙️ Central Governance & ESG Master Admin
  • Automated Carbon Offset Certificate: Computes CO2 averted and trees saved with verifiable QR links.
  • Corporate ESG Compliance: Real-time sustainability metrics for apartments & corporate offices.
  • Fraud Prevention & KYC: Real-time monitoring of user submissions and driver license verification.

04 Computer Vision AI Pipeline & Prompt Architecture

ECO connects directly to Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision API. Images submitted by citizens are validated through a zero-shot multi-parameter classification prompt:

POST /api/advanced/ai/scan-waste | Model: gemini-1.5-flash:generateContent { "wasteType": "Plastic", "materialSubtype": "PET Bottle #1", "confidenceScore": 0.96, "estimatedWeight": 1.5, "qualityScore": 92, "contaminationPct": 5, "remarks": "High-purity PET plastic bottle scan verified with 96% confidence." }
